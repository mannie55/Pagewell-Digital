## Decisions

- **1A Placeholder proof (decided: keep, block launch).** Testimonials, team cards and the "10+ years" / "50+ websites" stats keep their current layout and choreography. Replacing them with real, attributable content is a launch blocker: the site doesn't go public until each placeholder is real or removed.
- **1B Home lower half (decided: rewrite in place + link).** Keep the Services, About (pinned stage) and Process layouts; rewrite their copy to the growth positioning (find, choose, contact; no "AI-powered" lead). Each home Services card links to its service page. Team stays on home and /about.
- **2A Contact form fields (decided: extend the sentence form).** Keep the fill-in-the-blanks form. Add two optional blanks, "my website is [website]" and "or WhatsApp me on [number]", and include both in the enquiry email. Name, email and "a few things" stay required. Supersedes the memo's plain 5-field form.
- **2B 404 page (decided: branded).** `app/not-found.tsx` uses `PageHeader` ("This page isn't here"), one line of help, and links to Services, Work and Contact us. No search, no illustration.

- **4A Browser surfaces (decided: theme them, plus browser chrome colour).** `::selection` in Flamingo tints (flamingo-lighter on light sections, flamingo-dark/white text on dark), `caret-color` flamingo-dark in form fields, a thin neutral scrollbar, and `theme-color` #f56a2a via the root layout's `viewport` export so the address bar is Pagewell orange on Chrome/Edge mobile (Safari tints from it; Firefox ignores it).

- **5A DESIGN.md (decided: write it now).** Root `DESIGN.md` documenting the system as built (colour scales and their roles, type scale, spacing and gutters, radii, motion rules incl. where the blur reveal is allowed, motifs: halftone seam + dot field, component inventory, CTA hierarchy, video rules). It ships together with the mobile token revision raised by the founder in the same answer (see Pass 6) so it records the revised values, not today's cramped mobile ones.

- **5B Section label (decided: one shared component).** `SectionLabel` (light and dark variants, the pill spec from the design system) plus one section-header spacing rule (label → heading 16px, heading → intro 16px), used by every home section and `PageHeader`. The Work section's hand-made dark pill and the drifted `Tag` padding go.

- **6C Mobile token revision (decided: full revision).** Founder: "the current experience on mobile is really really bad". In `app/globals.css` and DESIGN.md:
  - `container-page` gutter 12px → 20px below md (hero card inset matches).
  - Headings fluid with `clamp()` between 375px and 1440px, no jump at md: H1 40→84px, H2 34→60px, H3 28→48px, H4 22→40px, H5 20→32px, H6 18→26px; line-height 1.1 for H1–H4 (was 1.0).
  - Phone section rhythm 56px top/bottom (`py-14`), card padding 20px.
  - Every tap target at least 44×44px (service pills, footer links, breadcrumb links, nav menu items).
  - Re-check every page at 375px and 768px after the change.
- **6D Long-word overflow (decided: wrap + full-width row).** A global `overflow-wrap: anywhere` for body text and links so long words wrap; on case studies the Live site fact spans both columns below md. No page may scroll sideways at 375px.
- **6A Split-word headings (decided: fix the markup).** Headings using the word reveal (`RevealHeading effect="words"` and the hero H1) carry their full text for screen readers (e.g. a visually hidden full-text copy or `aria-label` on a non-heading wrapper, whichever VoiceOver reads cleanly) with the word spans `aria-hidden`. Visual animation unchanged. Verify once with VoiceOver or TalkBack.
- **6B FAQ markup (decided: plain summary text).** The home FAQ's question text sits directly in `<summary>` (no `h3`), styled the same, matching the service-page FAQs.
- **7A Audit turnaround (decided: soften).** Every instance becomes "Free. Three written findings within a few working days." (bento CTA tile, Work strip, /contact note, memo and audit template). Tighten again only once one named person owns audit replies.

### R3: How long words wrap site-wide (T3)
Finding: C1, INFORMATIONAL, confidence 8/10, docs/designs/site-design-plan.md:55, reviewer: /plan-eng-review (Claude)
Plan baseline: Decision 6D (approved 2026-11-25): "A global `overflow-wrap: anywhere` for body text and links so long words wrap; on case studies the Live site fact spans both columns below md. No page may scroll sideways at 375px."
Runtime evidence: no global wrap rule today (only `break-all` on the success email at contact-form.tsx:26 and `[overflow-wrap:anywhere]` inside sentence-form.tsx:2488). CSS Text 3: `anywhere` adds its break points to an element's min-content width; `break-word` wraps the same overflowing words but leaves min-content alone. Flex and grid items sized by min-content (the stats `grid grid-cols-3` at social-proof-section.tsx:44, the two hero notch buttons, Tag pills) can therefore shrink and split short words mid-word under a global `anywhere`. Tailwind 4.3.3 ships `wrap-break-word` and `wrap-anywhere`.
Comparison grid:
